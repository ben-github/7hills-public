Plan Your Experience

What Do I Wear?

Audience members wear casual clothing such as jeans or dress shirts. Concerts take place on Sunday afternoons. Orchestra members may be in suits and dress pants, but formal attire is not required for audience members.

How Long Is the Concert?

Concerts are typically 90 minutes to 2 hours with an intermission of 10–15 minutes.

When Do I Clap?

Hearty applause is welcomed at the conclusion of pieces. We're not purists — you can clap between movements if inspired. Please no talking or eating during performances.

What About My Phone?

Silence or turn off your phone during the show. Photography and short video clips are permitted. We encourage you to share on social media — tag us @7HillsSymphony.
